How It Works

1. Authorization

The client provides the appropriate authorization, mandate, or service agreement that allows Ethio Link to assist with the assigned matter.

2. Assignment

Ethio Link reviews the client's needs and clearly defines the scope and responsibilities of the assigned matter.

3. Coordination

Ethio Link coordinates the assigned matter with the appropriate institutions, organizations, and stakeholders in Ethiopia.

4. Follow-Up

Ethio Link follows the progress of the assigned matter and maintains ongoing communication with the relevant parties.

5. Update

Ethio Link keeps the client informed with clear updates about the progress and current status of the assignment.

6. Completion

Once the assigned work is completed, Ethio Link provides the client with the final status and any relevant information related to the completed assignment.
